# Love Cast ❤️🍿
> **Your private, zero-latency cinema for two.** Watch YouTube movies, romantic songs, and videos together in real-time sync with floating reactions.

🌐 **Live App:** [https://lovecast-eight.vercel.app/](https://lovecast-eight.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

- ⚡ **Real-Time Playback Sync:** Play, pause, and seek stay synchronized down to the millisecond across both screens.
- 🔒 **Private Cinema Rooms:** Every room is secured with a custom 4-digit PIN to keep your viewing private.
- 📱 **Call-Friendly Streaming:** Built with inline player configurations so standard mobile voice/video calls never interrupt your stream.
- 💖 **Floating Emoji Reactions:** Send instant reactions (❤️, 😂, 🍿, 🥺) that float across both screens in real-time.
- 🔗 **One-Click Join:** Share a direct room link for seamless, instant pairing.
- 🚫 **No Sign-Up Required:** No account creation, passwords, or emails needed—just enter a nickname and start watching.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Create a Room:**
   - Open [Love Cast](https://lovecast-eight.vercel.app/)
   - Enter your **Nickname** and set a **4-Digit Room PIN**.
   - Click **Create Love Cast Room**.

2. **Invite Your Partner:**
   - Copy the room link or share your **Room ID** and **PIN**.
   - When your partner enters the PIN, the status pill will turn green: `🟢 Both Connected`.

3. **Enjoy Together:**
   - Paste any YouTube link into the search bar and click **Load Movie**.
   - Control playback together and send floating emoji reactions anytime!

> 💡 **Tip:** For the best audio experience during voice calls, plug in your earphones!

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React (Vite) + Tailwind CSS + Lucide Icons
- **Realtime Sync Engine:** Firebase Realtime Database
- **Video Engine:** YouTube IFrame Player API
- **Deployment:** Vercel
